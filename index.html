About the Team

Father and Son, Working Together

Scott Smith

Realtor, Seven Gables Real Estate

Scott Smith is a licensed REALTOR® with Seven Gables Real Estate's Orange office, serving buyers and sellers across Central and North Orange County. Licensed since 2005, he has more than 20 years in the business and over 400 transactions behind him, and is a consistent top producer. Before real estate, Scott spent 18 years as a manufacturing technician. A U.S. military veteran, he supports Tunnels to Towers and local Veterans Day efforts. He works alongside his son, Nick, bringing clients two generations of local knowledge and experience.

Nick Smith

Realtor, Seven Gables Real Estate

Nick Smith is a licensed REALTOR® with Seven Gables Real Estate's Orange office, serving first-time homebuyers and young families across North and South Orange County. A Fullerton native, Nick brings authentic local knowledge along with 12 years of hospitality experience. That career sharpened his ability to understand people, communicate clearly, and stay calm under pressure. He works as a father-son team with his dad, a 22-year veteran Seven Gables agent, giving clients fresh energy paired with deep expertise. Nick leads with honesty and a commitment to being his clients' calm in the storm.
