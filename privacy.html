Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scott and Nick Smith, operating as part of Seven Gables Real Estate ("we," "us," or "our"), collect, use, and protect information when you visit this website or use its features, including our contact form, home value estimate tool, property search, and email newsletter signup.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you voluntarily provide it through a form on this site:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property search criteria and address information (for home value estimates)
  • Any message or details you choose to share with us

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this site is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested information about properties, buying, or selling
  • Provide home value estimates and property search results
  • Send occasional updates or listings if you sign up for our newsletter
  • Improve our services and this website

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

This site uses RealScout, a third-party real estate technology platform, to power property search, home value estimate, and lead management features. When you use these tools, information you enter is shared with RealScout in order to provide these services and may be used to contact you regarding your inquiry. Please refer to RealScout's own privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.
